The short version

Search for the project's name today and more than ten broker and portal pages answer before any official source. Among them they give three developer names, four possession dates and two land areas. Google and AI answer engines can't verify which is true, so they hedge or cite brokers. Meanwhile the project's real advantage, both villa types under ₹2 Cr inside a gated community of 47 homes, is said nowhere with authority.
