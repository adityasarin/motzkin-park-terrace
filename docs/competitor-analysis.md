**Reference sites studied for features:** Supreme Villagio (Pune): landscape story, awards, EMI calculator, RERA QR, a "beware look-alike sites" banner. Daiwik Sayuri: courtyard story, gated price, interactive landmark map, detailed specification sheet.

## What every competitor site does
A hero with the price and RERA number; a gated cost sheet, brochure and plans; an amenities icon grid; connectivity distances; FAQ; RERA and disclaimer footer.

## What they get wrong (our opening)
1. **Inconsistent facts:** prices, distances and unit counts differ across pages and portals. Our `/official-facts` page and single `project.ts` source fix this for us.
2. **No structured data:** none of the reviewed sites carried JSON-LD. Ours carries Organization, ApartmentComplex, Offer, RealEstateListing, FAQPage and Breadcrumb.
3. **Broker microsites own the brand keyword.** We will run a brand ad group, claim the directories and ask brokers to link to the official site.

## Where we win, and where we don't
| We win on | We concede |
|---|---|
| Price: both villa types under ₹2 Cr | Facilities: no 45k sq ft club, no forest |
| Certainty: RERA-registered and under construction, not pre-launch | Brand recognition |
| Boutique scale: 47 homes, a quiet club | Land per villa in the largest plotted townships |
| One honest source of facts | Ad budget |

**The line to use:** "A gated villa on Sarjapur–Attibele Road, under ₹2 crore, in a community small enough to know your neighbours."
